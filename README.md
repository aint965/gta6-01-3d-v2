# Palkon Auto

A hand-built WebGL 2 city for Make GTA 6. Walk Solmar's seven coastal districts, follow its canal and marina, and explore the park through scorching daylight, golden hour, and a star-filled night. Its city, architecture, characters, and story are original.

Run `node serve.ts` and open `http://localhost:8080`. Walk with WASD, run with Shift, choose a district in the top rail, and orbit or zoom with the mouse. Use the Day, Sunset, and Night controls (or D, S, and N) to change the city lighting.
