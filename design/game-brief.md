# Palkon Auto: Stage A Brief

Palkon Auto is an original, sunlit open-world crime game set in Solmar, a coastal city measuring 3,840 by 3,456 units. Its seven districts are Old Solmar, Canal Ward, South Market, Citrus Quarter, Pearl Marina, Glasspoint, and Sunstrand Beach.

Coastal courier Mara Venn uncovers a private security racket run by Bastion Civic and its broker, Silas Rook. Archivist Neri and organizer Oren help her expose it. All game text is English. Names, art, and fonts are original; the project uses no Rockstar marks or assets and copies no commercial game.

The eight missions, in order, are: 1. A Receipt for Sunshine; 2. Salt Ledger; 3. After the Shutters; 4. Glass Debt; 5. The Long Way Home; 6. Unbought Ground; 7. Open Contract; 8. Everyone Gets the Morning.

Zero health triggers recovery, a cash penalty, and mission failure. Arrest ends an attempt with a cash penalty. A timer, boundary, destroyed vehicle, lost evidence, or incapacitated ally fails the active mission, which can be retried.

The visual palette is limestone `#ead8b2`, coral `#d77f69`, teal `#398e99`, ink `#142d38`, and leaf `#56856b`.

## Six-stage plan

- Stage A (this build): handwritten WebGL renderer, deterministic city, lighting cycle, and park.
- Stage B: driving, traffic, pedestrians, traffic lights, HUD, menus, saves, and sound.
- Stage C: missions one through four.
- Stage D: missions five through eight.
- Stage E: police, combat, garage, radio, world-life density, and brightness/contrast tuning.
- Stage F: screenshots including the title screen, final verification, and report.

This build implements Stage A only. Day/night world captures are included for development verification; the title screen and final screenshot set remain for Stage F.
